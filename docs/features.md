# Features at a glance

Safety data sheets are the mandatory documents for products such as paints,
adhesives or cleaners: they say how hazardous a product is and how to handle
it safely. sdb-reader turns these PDF files into reliable data.

- Reads thousands of files automatically, scanned ones too
- Recognises whether a file is a German safety data sheet at all
- Finds hazard warnings and symbols, even where they are printed only as
  pictures
- Recognises product, manufacturer and date
- Recognises outdated sheets that are no longer valid
- Reports errors in a sheet, but only where they are certain
- Does not pass on incomplete hazard warnings unless asked to
- Same file, same result, every time
- Every result can be traced back
- Reads without AI and without a cloud, on your own computer
- Results for programs or as a readable report, in German or English
- Open source
