# Features at a glance

The short list. Each point is told in more detail, with the rules behind
it, in [What it handles](what-it-handles.md).
Deutsch: [Funktionen auf einen Blick](funktionen.md).

## The file

- Tells a German safety data sheet from a safety information, a sheet in
  another language and any other document
- Several sheets in one file, each read on its own
- Cover pages skipped
- Scanned sheets and single scanned pages
- Text put back in printed order: tables, columns, broken words
- Section headings in many forms, also the order from before REACH
- Embedded eSDScom XML read

## Old rules and new

- Three kinds of sheet: before June 2017, from June 2017, under 2020/878
- Old sheets read without their label, with findings on their age only
- R and S phrases never turned into codes
- Orange symbols of the old directives not taken for pictograms

## The label

- Label headings in many wordings
- Page heads and feet ignored
- Separate labels for packages of at most 125 ml
- Separate blocks under the Biocidal Products and Detergents Regulations
- Conditions such as "for consumers" kept
- Codes assigned to statements printed without their code, and marked so
- Statements with a gap, combined statements, code lists, codes with spaces
- The sheet's wording beside the regulation's
- Classification entries and running text kept out of the label

## Pictograms

- Read as code, as picture, or both, and says which
- Red, black and grey frames
- Pictograms drawn in strips, and pictograms that touch
- Pictograms on scanned pages
- Small pictograms looked at again in finer detail
- Flame and flame over circle told apart
- Codes misread by text recognition put right

## Product, supplier and dates

- Classification as stated in section 2
- Product name, supplier, address, phone, e-mail, website
- Emergency number apart from the supplier's phone
- The German firm where a sheet names several
- Dates in many formats; a date in the future is no date
- Version and countries (DE, AT, CH)
- The current sheet among several of one product

## Findings

- A classified product without a label
- A statement missing from the label, or one the classification does not name
- A signal word that contradicts the codes
- A pictogram that should give way to another
- A statement worded otherwise than the regulation
- A sheet due for renewal
- Sheets of different products in one file
- Only where the reader is sure

## Safe by default

- An incomplete label is given out only on request
- "Does the product need a sheet?": yes, no, or not checked under the rules
  in force
- The same file always gives the same result
- No language model, no clock, works offline
